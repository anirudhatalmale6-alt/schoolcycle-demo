Safe for buyers. Fair for sellers. Funding for schools.

SchoolCycle holds the payment in escrow until the buyer is happy, and routes a commission back to the school on every sale. Here's how each part works.

🛍️ Buying an item

1
Find it

Search by school, category, size, brand, condition or price and open a listing.

2
Pay securely

Pay by card, Instant EFT or mobile. Your money goes into SchoolCycle escrow — not straight to the seller.

3
Receive it

Collect from the seller, use a courier, or have it dropped at the school office.

4
Confirm & release

Confirm you're happy and the seller is paid. If you go quiet, funds auto-release after 5 days — with a dispute option first.

🏷️ Selling an item

1
List free in minutes

Add 2–6 photos, a description, price, size, brand, condition and the school. No approval needed.

2
Get an order

Buyers pay upfront into escrow, so you know the money is there before you hand anything over.

3
Hand over

Meet the buyer, courier it, or drop it at the school office for the buyer to collect.

4
Get paid

Once the buyer confirms, your earnings land in your SchoolCycle wallet — withdraw to your bank anytime.

🏫 School drop-off (optional)

1
Seller chooses drop-off

At checkout the seller can offer "drop at school office" as a handover option.

2
School receives it

The school office logs into its dashboard and marks the item "received & ready for collection".

3
Buyer is notified

The buyer gets an alert that their item is waiting at the school office.

4
Collected & closed

Buyer collects and confirms — the seller is paid and the school records another handled sale.

💚 How schools earn

1
Onboard the school

We add the school with its logo, categories and banking details for commission payouts.

2
Parents sell & buy

Every item linked to the school counts toward that school's earnings.

3
Commission is set aside

A share of the service fee on each sale is automatically allocated to the school.

4
School sees its income

The school dashboard shows sales, commission earned and payouts — real funding, automatically.
