How SchoolCycle works

Simple, safe and good for your school.

Full guide →
1

List in minutes

Snap a few photos, pick your school, set a price and condition. No approval needed.

2

Buy safely

Pay online by card or Instant EFT. We hold the money securely in escrow.

3

Collect or deliver

Collect, courier, or drop at the school office — the school confirms receipt.

4

Everyone wins

You confirm you're happy, the seller gets paid, and the school earns its cut.

Money held safely. Schools supported.

SchoolCycle holds every payment in escrow until the buyer confirms the item arrived and they're happy — then the seller is paid and the school earns its commission. Safe for buyers, fair for sellers, funding for schools.
